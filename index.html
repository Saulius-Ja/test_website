NordicPulse is a Nordic electronics manufacturing services (EMS) company with its factory in Kaunas, Lithuania. We combine Nordic standards for quality and openness with the cost efficiency of a Baltic production site, and build for customers across the product life cycle, from development and industrialisation to series production, logistics and repair, all from a single factory where every engineer is a short walk from the line.
